## What is this?

This is a collection of around 600 high quality recreations of Portal 2 assets that I've made over the past few months. Everything here was made from scratch in either Blender or Substance Designer/Painter. This will be updated continuously as I make more assets. 

If anything is broken, or if you have any feedback, or if you want to commission me for assets outside of the scope of the repository, feel free to reach out to me on Discord (lenship2). 

No AI was used for the creation of any of these assets :)

## Credits:
These assets were made by almost entirely by me (with the exception of a handful of models being made by Hazel Webs and textured by me), so when using them in a map you should credit as such. If you want to use them in a larger scale mod, please contact me for permission first!
